Karate Belt Progression (JKA Style)

10th Kyu – White Belt

Kihon: Basic stances (Zenkutsu-dachi, Kokutsu-dachi), Oi-zuki, Age-uke

Kata: Taikyoku Shodan

Kumite: Basic one-step sparring

9th Kyu – Orange Belt

Kihon: Mae-geri, Soto-uke, Shuto-uke

Kata: Taikyoku Nidan

Kumite: One-step sparring with defense

8th Kyu – Red Belt

Kihon: Gyaku-zuki, Yoko-geri keage

Kata: Heian Shodan

Kumite: Ippon kumite with counters

7th Kyu – Yellow Belt

Kihon: Mae-geri kekomi, Uchi-uke

Kata: Heian Nidan

Kumite: Jiyu-ippon kumite basics

6th Kyu – Green Belt

Kihon: Yoko-geri kekomi, Mawashi-geri

Kata: Heian Sandan

Kumite: Jiyu-ippon kumite (attack + defense)

5th Kyu – Blue Belt

Kihon: Ushiro-geri, Kizami-zuki

Kata: Heian Yondan

Kumite: Jiyu kumite basics

4th Kyu – Purple Belt

Kihon: Combinations of kicks + punches

Kata: Heian Godan

Kumite: Free sparring drills

3rd–1st Kyu – Brown Belt

Kihon: Advanced combinations, footwork

Kata: Tekki Shodan, Bassai Dai, Kanku Dai

Kumite: Jiyu kumite (free sparring)

Shodan (1st Dan) – Black Belt

Kihon: Mastery of fundamentals + advanced drills

Kata: Bassai Dai, Jion, Empi

Kumite: Full free sparring with strategy
